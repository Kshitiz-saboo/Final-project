Our Story

A Legacy of Sweetness Since 1987

Founded by the Patel family in the heart of Surat, Gujarat, Surati Sweets began as a small mithai shop with a simple mission: to preserve and celebrate the authentic flavors of traditional Indian sweets while embracing innovation.

For over three decades, we've been crafting exquisite sweets using time-honored recipes passed down through generations. What started as a family passion has blossomed into a beloved destination for sweet lovers across Gujarat.

Today, under the leadership of Rajesh Patel, we continue to blend tradition with creativity, offering both classic mithai and contemporary fusion desserts that delight modern palates while respecting our rich culinary heritage.
